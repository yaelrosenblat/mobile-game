## Core Loop
A supermarket simulator where players take charge of running and expanding their own store. Restock shelves with a variety of products by completing different mini-games. Successfully completing mini-games rewards players with coins and XP, which they can use to purchase new items and upgrades from the game shop. As players earn XP and level up, they unlock new products, expand their supermarket's selection, and receive additional rewards. Players can also use optional in-app purchases to earn double rewards or gain extra attempts, allowing them to progress faster without waiting.

## Meta
Gradually grow and improve your store by unlocking new Products. Earn XP to level up and unlock new products, rewards, and features. Purchases for double rewards, extra mini-game attempts, or faster progression. Complete milestones and challenges to earn special rewards and unlock new content. Spend coins on new items, improvements, and upgrades that make running the supermarket more rewarding.

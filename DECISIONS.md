## 2026-11-04 — Refined the Game Direction

**Decision:** Develop a hybrid-casual grocery store game focused on collecting products through chance-based mini-games and arranging them on shelves.

**Core loop:** Play a mini-game → receive products → arrange and fill shelves → complete goals → earn coins and XP → expand the store.

**Mini-games:** A prize wheel, a slot machine, and a scratch card. Each awards virtual product types and quantities.

**Why:** This direction combines short, simple interactions and surprise rewards with visible progress as players fill and develop their store. It also provides opportunities to build shared reward systems, inventory management, progression, and local saving.

**Proposed scope:** Three mini-games, one store screen, four store progression milestones, coins and XP, unlockable shelves and products, store upgrades, and local saving. The final scope requires instructor approval.

**Platform and controls:** Android, portrait orientation, playable with one thumb, with sessions lasting 1–3 minutes.

**AI assistance:** Used ChatGPT to help clarify the pitch and document the game direction.

**Team:** Yael Rosenblat, Chen Weiss, May Solomon.
